Clean-Up Crew is an asymmetric team vs monster survival horror experience for the Zandronum source port, inspired by the Half-life 2 mod The Hidden: Source.

A team of corporate liquidators, known as The Cleaners, are tasked with fighting a monster known only as The Threat, a creature that is invisible to the naked eye.
Each human player is separated into three distinct classes, but there can only be one monster at a time. Whomever can defeat The Threat will play them at the beginning of the next round.
The Threat will feed, skulk, hide, and take out each and every Cleaner one by one until there is no one left standing. Or die trying.
